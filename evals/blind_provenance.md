# Blind question provenance

The 10 questions in `blind_questions.csv` were written by a classmate who had not seen the index. They form an independently authored blind set, separate from my 20 diagnostic questions.

The blind set contains questions S01–S10, with an expected answer anchor and source for each question. The results files retain the recorded model answers and scores.

The SHA-256 manifest records the current question files for reproducibility. Do not tune on these ten questions in subsequent work; use fresh blind questions for further final evaluation.
